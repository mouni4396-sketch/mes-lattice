Unrestrict Production Order#

🔒 ProductionOrder.Unrestrict

Overview#

This operation reverts the effects of restricting a Production Order. An unrestricted Production Order can have Materials added to and removed from it.

Setup#

No special setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Production Order must be active and must not be in the state Closed.
  • The Production Order must be restricted.

Sequence of Steps#

This screen captures all the necessary information to complete the operation.

  1. Review the Production Order details.
  2. Press Unrestrict to complete the transaction.
