Manage Repair Policy#

🔒 Product.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to manage the Repair Policy of the Product by defining which type of defects can be repaired or not.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Product exists and it is Active.

Sequence of Steps#

The Manage Repair Policy wizard can be called from the Product page by selecting Repair Policy from the section menus on the right, and then selecting the Manage button.

Step 1: Repair Policy#

  1. Define the Defects Repair Policy, which can be:

    • Allow All (default) - repair is possible for all Defect Reasons.
    • Allow Specific - repair is possible only for the associated Product Defects.
    • Forbid All - no repair is possible for all Defect Reasons.
    • Forbid Specific - repair is not possible for the associated Product Defects.

  2. If you select Allow All or Forbid all, you can Update to complete the operation.

Step 2: Repair Reasons#

  1. If you select Allow Specific or Forbid Specific, you must provide at least one Reason of type Defect. Use to add as many defect reasons of type Defect as you need and to delete reasons as needed.
  2. Select Update to complete the operation.
