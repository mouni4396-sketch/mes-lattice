Preview Rules#

🔒 Product.PreviewCharacteristicRules

Overview#

This operation is used to preview the Rules of the Characteristics of the Product.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • Product exists in the system and it is not Terminated.
  • Product has at least one Product Characteristic Rule.
  • Product has at least one Product Characteristic.

Sequence of Steps#

The Preview Rules wizard can be called from the Product page by selecting Characteristics and Rules from the menu section on the right, and then selecting the Rules tab.

Step 1: Characteristics#

  1. Complete any mandatory Product Characteristics information. Characteristics are presented in the order defined, and their values must come from the associated Lookup Table or be entered as free text.

  2. Select Next to continue.

Step 2: Preview#

  1. Preview the results of the Rules Evaluation.
