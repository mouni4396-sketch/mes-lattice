Manage Safety Information#

🔒 Product.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to define and manage the Safety Information definitions for the Product.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Product exists and it is Active.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1: Manage Safety Information#

Navigate to the Safety Information section in the main Product page and press the Manage button to access the configuration wizard:

  1. Specify which Routes of Exposure are applicable:

    • Inhalation
    • Skin Absorption
    • Ingestion
    • Injection

  2. Specify the following hazard information:

    • Compressed Gas
    • Flammable Material
    • Oxidizing
    • Explosion Hazard
    • Fatal
    • Biohazardous / Infectious
    • Corrosive
    • Health Hazard
    • Harmful
    • Harmful to the Environment

  3. Specify the following required protection equipment:

    • Safety Glasses
    • Chemical Goggles
    • Face Shield
    • Dust Mask
    • Vapor Respirator
    • Breathing Apparatus
    • Gloves
    • Synthetic Apron
    • Protective Clothing
    • Boots

  4. Specify the following hazard classifications:

    • Fire: Will Not Burn; Must Be Preheated To Burn; Ignites When Moderately Heated; Ignites At Normal Temperatures; Extremely Flammable
    • Health: No Hazard; Slight hazardous; Hazardous;Extreme danger; Deadly
    • Instability: Stable, Unstable If Heated, Violent Chemical Change, Shock Or Heat May Detonate, May Detonate
    • Specific: Acid; Alkali; Corrosive; Oxidizer; Radioactive; Use No Water

  5. Select Update to complete the operation.

Info

Product Technical Sheets and Safety Data Sheets can be further added as attachments, defining the attachment types as the ones defined in the following configuration entries:

  • /Cmf/System/Configuration/Product/SafetyDataSheet - defaults to "Safety Data Sheet"

  • /Cmf/System/Configuration/Product/TechnicalDataSheet - defaults to "Technical Data Sheet"
