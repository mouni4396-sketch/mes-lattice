Manage Product Manufacturers#

🔒 Product.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to define the Business Partners (Manufacturers) associated to this Product.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Product exists and it is Active.
  • The Product Type is Raw Material, Part or Durable.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1: Manage Product Manufacturers#

Navigating to the Product Manufacturer section on the right hand side of the screen will scroll the page to the appropriate section and the Manage Product Manufacturers wizard can be opened by pressing the Manage button.

  1. Add as many Product Manufacturers as desired, providing for each line:
    • The Business Partner name.
    • The Part Number.
    • Optionally choose the Moisture Sensitivity Level for the Product Manufacturer. The list of possible values is taken from the lookup table MoistureSensitivityLevel. The values for the Floor Life and Floor Life Unit of Time will be displayed automatically.
    • Optionally specify the Capacity Class (from the lookup table CapacityClass). This property is only used for scheduling purposes for Resources of type Tunnel and Batch.
    • Optionally define the Standard Quantity of the supplied part.
    • Optionally leave a note specific to the Manufacturer.
  2. Select Update to complete the operation.

Warning

If there are more than one Product Manufacturer defined for a single Product, the combination between Business Partner and Part Number must be unique.
