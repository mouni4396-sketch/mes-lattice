Mark Task As Completed#

🔒 Task.Perform

Overview#

This operation marks a Task as completed (done).

Setup#

No special setup is required other than to meet the preconditions.

Preconditions#

  • The Task must be Active.
  • The Task must be in the state Released or In Progress.

Sequence of Steps#

  1. Pressing the Mark As Completed button in the toolbar will complete the operation.
