Special Deliver Material Transfer#

🔒 MaterialTransfer.SpecialDeliver

Overview#

This operation is used to deliver a Material Transfer. This transaction is different from the Deliver Material Transfer as it does not require an electronic signature and it bypasses some security restrictions.

Setup#

In order for the Deliver Material Transfer to be fully functional, one customization must be deployed in Critical Manufacturing's installation:

  • To resolve the Materials from the Source Facility to where Quantity is taken for mode ConsumeInventory.

Preconditions#

  • The Material Transfer's universal state is Active and its system state is Approved Or Ready.
  • The Material Transfer's TransferMode must be ConsumeInventory or ConsumeMaterial.
  • The Facility, Area and Step where the Materials will be received must be the same for all Material Transfer Materials and must match the Material Transfer's ToFacility.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1: Details#

  1. Select the Material Transfer and press the Deliver button to initialize the wizard.
  2. Review the Material Transfer's information.
  3. If not yet prepared, then:
  4. For Material Transfer mode ConsumeMaterial or TransferMaterial: provide one or more Materials and respective quantities to fulfill the Material Transfer. Use the and buttons in the grid's toolbar to add and remove Materials.
  5. For Material Transfer modes ConsumeInventory or TransferInventory: specify the quantity to be transferred.
  6. Optionally, enter a comment.
  7. Click Special Deliver to complete the transaction.
