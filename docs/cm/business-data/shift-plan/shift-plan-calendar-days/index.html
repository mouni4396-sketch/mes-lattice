Manage Shift Plan Calendar Days#

🔒 ShiftPlan.ManageCalendarDays

🔒 ShiftPlan.SpecialManageCalendarDays

Warning

The system will automatically use the highest possible privilege. The difference between the Manage and the Special Manage is that the Manage checks that the current user belongs to the owner role of the Workgroup or the owner role of the Shift Plan.

Overview#

The Shift Plan Calendar Days are accessible through the Calendar View in the Shift Plan page. Due to performance reasons, the Calendar Days are only actually loaded once at least one Workgroup has been selected from the filters on the top. At most, only one month can be visible at any time.
