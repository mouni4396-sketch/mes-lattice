Manage Compatible Services#

🔒 Service.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to manage the Services that are compatible with the current Service, that is, the Services that can replace or be replaced by the current Service.

Info

Compatible services are services with identical Processing Type. They are directional and not reciprocal (for a valid example, a Resource can process certain Materials for longer times than others).

Warning

The Service cannot be compatible with itself.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Service exists and it is Active in the system.

Sequence of Steps#

Can be Fulfilled by#

  1. Add, edit or remove the Services that can replace the current Service by selecting the or remove buttons on the top right-hand side of the grid.
  2. Confirm the details of the Service being displayed on the right-hand side of the screen.
  3. Select Update to confirm the operation.

Can Fulfill#

  1. Add, edit or remove the Services that can be replaced by the current Service by selecting the or remove buttons on the top right-hand side of the grid.
  2. Confirm the details of the Service being displayed on the right-hand side of the screen.
  3. Select Update to confirm the operation.
