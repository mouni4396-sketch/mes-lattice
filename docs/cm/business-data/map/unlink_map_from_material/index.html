Unlink Map from Material#

🔒 Material.UnlinkFromMap

Overview#

This operation is used to unlink a Map from a Material.

Setup#

No setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Map and the Material exist and are active.
  • The Map to be removed is associated with the Material.
  • If the Map being unlinked is the current Material Master Map, no other Material Maps must be associated. The Material Master Map must be the last Map to be removed from the Material.

Sequence of Steps#

From the Map perspective#

  1. Select the Map from which you wish to unlink the Material.
  2. Click Unlink.
  3. Verify the Material information.
  4. Optionally, enter a comment.
  5. Press Unlinkto complete the transaction.

From the Material perspective#

  1. The existing linked Maps will be listed under Linked Maps.
  2. Click the Unlink button on the ribbon.
  3. Once the wizard is open, select the map(s) you wish to unlink from the Material by checking the respective checkbox(es).
  4. Optionally, enter a comment.
  5. Press Unlink to complete the operation.
