Manage Step Inspection Severity Instances#

🔒 Step.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to Manage Inspection Severity Instances of the Step.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Step exists and it is Active.
  • The Enable Inspections field in the Manage Inspection Information Settings is true.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1#

  1. Navigate to the Inspections section in the main Step details page:

  2. In the Manage button drop-down, select Manage Inspection Severity Instances:

  3. Add, edit, or remove Severity Instances (use to add, and to remove) as needed.

  4. Provide the Severity (mandatory) for each instance according to what is defined in the InspectionSeverity lookup table:

  5. Enter other information as needed.

  6. Select Update to complete the operation.

The Severity Instances information will be available in the appropriate tab:
