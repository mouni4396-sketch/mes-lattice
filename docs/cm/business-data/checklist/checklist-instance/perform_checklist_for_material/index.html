Perform Immediate Checklist for Material#

🔒 ChecklistInstance.PerformImmediateForMaterial

Overview#

Accessible from the Checklist details page, performs a standalone Immediate Checklist for a given Material. In this case, you do not need to Open Checklist Instance for Material.

Setup#

No special setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Material exists, is Active and non-template.
  • The Material is not In Transit.
  • The Checklist is of scope Material Tracking and the Execution mode is Immediate.

Sequence of Steps#

To execute an Immediate Checklist for a Material, it's necessary to:

  1. Find the desired Checklist instance and select Perform New.

  2. Execute the Checklist items and when you have finished, select Perform to conclude the operation.

Info

For more information on how to execute the Checklist items, see Perform Checklist Instance.
