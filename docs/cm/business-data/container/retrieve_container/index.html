Retrieve Container#

🔒 Container.Retrieve

Overview#

This operation is used to remove a Container from a storage Resource.

Setup#

No special setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Container exists, it is Active and its state is Available.
  • The Container is in Store, i.e., the Resource Association Type is Stored Container.
  • The storage Resource exists and is Active.

Sequence of Steps#

  1. Select the Container(s) to be retrieved by checking the respective checkbox(es).
  2. Press Retrieve to complete the transaction.

Info

Depending on the Future Actions' configuration, the Material may be put on hold.
