Add Images to Reason#

🔒 Reason.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to associate the Loss Reason with as many images of Loss scope as desired.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the precondition of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • There are active Multimedia objects of type Picture and scope Loss available in the system.
  • The Reason exists and is Active in the system.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1 - Images#

The Add Images to Reason operation can be called from the Reason page within the Business Data Group, scrolling to the Images section in the main Reason page. In the wizard:

  1. Add as many images as desired from the available images displayed on the dialog.
  2. Select Next to continue to the next step.

Step 2 - Assessment#

  1. For each of the images selected in the previous step, define an Assessment from the available options:

    • Conditional
    • Fail
    • Pass
    • Unspecified

  2. Optionally, enter complementary information in the Assessment Criteria field.

  3. Select Add to complete the transaction.
