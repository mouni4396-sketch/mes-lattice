Confirm Transfer Order Items#

🔒 TransferOrder.ConfirmItems

🔒 TransferOrder.ConfirmItemsBySelection

Overview#

This operation is used to confirm the items of a Transfer Order after it completes the movement. The confirmation process will execute, for every Transfer Order item:

  • Create new Materials if the Transfer Order Item Type is ProductQuantity and the Product Material Logistics Mode is Material
  • Store the Material in appropriate Storage Locations

Setup#

No special setup is required other than to meet the preconditions.

Preconditions#

  • The Transfer Orders are Active and in state Delivered.
  • All selected Transfer Orders are for the same Destination Location.

Sequence of Steps#

Step 1#

  1. From the Transfer Order page or from the list of Transfer Orders, press the Confirm button on the top ribbon.
  2. Select or scan each of the items in the list and mark them as picked. Depending on the role and the security features associated to the current user, the confirmation can be made through selection or through scanning of each individual item.
  3. If the item is of Material type, the Material is already selected and the item will be confirmed. However, if the item is of ProductQuantity type, individual Materials will have to be created until the Product quantity is matched. Pressing the button will add another Material, with an automatically generated name. Pressing the button will remove the Material. For each added Material, selecting it will open the details panel for the Material, where all editable main properties for the Material can be edited, including the proper Primary Quantity that will be a part of the Transfer Order item. When the full quantity is complete, the item can be confirmed.

  1. Optionally, define additional information:

  2. Supplier

  3. Purchase Order Number
  4. Manufacturer
  5. Manufacturer Part Number
  6. Manufacturer Lot Number
  7. Manufacturer Date Code
  8. Expiration Date
  9. RoHS compliance
  10. Moisture Sensitivity Level
  11. Floor Life

  12. Floor Life Units

  13. Select the Destination Storage location for each Material.

  14. Press the Complete button to complete the operation.

Info

If the Step where the Material is currently placed has the Print Material Labels Automatically property set to true, that information will be displayed on the bottom section of the wizard.
