Material Cost History Report#

🔒 Material.CostHistoryReport

Description#

This report provides access to the Material Cost History report of any Material which is available in the Operational Data Store (ODS) database.

Data Source(s)#

  • ODS database

Filters#

  • None

Fields#

  • Facility
  • Step
  • Step Cycle Time (in hours)
  • Service
  • Product
  • Resource where the Material was last processed
  • Operations performed, displaying for each operation:
    • Name
    • Date and time when the operation was performed
    • User that performed the operation
    • Primary Quantity of the Main Material involved
    • Primary Quantity of the Sub-Materials involved
    • Old Material Cost of the Main Material involved
    • Old Material Cost of the Sub-Materials involved
    • New Material Cost of the Main Material involved
    • New Material Cost of the Sub-Materials involved
    • Material Delta Cost of the Main Material involved
    • Material Delta Cost of the Sub-Materials involved

Example#

Note

The configuration entry Cmf/System/Configuration/Reporting/Reports/DetailedMaterialHistory/HoursToInclude allows you to define the default date time range interval of the report.
