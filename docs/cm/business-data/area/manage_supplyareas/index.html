Manage Supply Areas#

🔒 Area.Edit

Overview#

This operation is used to manage the Supply Areas for an existing Area.

Setup#

No specific setup is required other than to meet the preconditions of the transaction.

Preconditions#

  • The Area exists in the system.
  • The Area is of AreaType StorageArea.

Sequence of Steps#

The Manage Supply Areas wizard can be called from the Area page within the Business Data Group, scrolling to the Supply Areas section in the main Area page. In the wizard:

  1. On the left side panel, add as many rows as desired (each row represents a new Supply Area to be added to the currently selected Area) by pressing in the top right of the grid and pressing to remove any added entry.
  2. Provide a Name for the new Supply Area.
  3. Select the Area to be used as a Supply Area.
  4. Press Update to complete the transaction.
